## Model-vs-reality boundary (standing)

The CBF filter models a **point** end-effector, **spherical** obstacles, and an
**axis-aligned** workspace box, in **discrete time** (continuous-time-inspired
barrier, enforced by the next-state validator). It is not a dynamics-level CBF,
not a forward-invariance certificate, and not a substitute for Franka Desk, a
physical E-stop, a dead-man control, and lab procedures. Defaults remain
`use_robot=false`, `dry_run=true`, physical output disabled, stop on solver
failure, hold-then-stop on stale/invalid input.
